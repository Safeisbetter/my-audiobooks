How listening works

  1. 1 Press play in your browser. Nothing needs installing.
  2. 2 Download a book to keep it on your device.
  3. 3 Your place isn't saved; the site keeps no record of you.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, loads no trackers, and runs no third-party scripts. Its content security policy blocks anything it doesn't need. The hosting provider still handles normal web requests, as any host does.

Add a book

The owner adds new titles by uploading an MP3 to the audio folder and listing it in books.json. Only public-domain or openly licensed books are published here.
